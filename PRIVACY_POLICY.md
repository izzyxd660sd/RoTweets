# RoTweet Fresh Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 9, 2026

RoTweet Fresh adds a small sidebar to Roblox experience pages and displays publicly available posts associated with a suggested or user-entered X account.

## Data stored

The extension stores only its settings: game-ID-to-X-username mappings and an optional default X username. These settings are saved using Chrome's `storage.sync` facility. The extension does not ask for a Roblox password, X password, or account login.

## Network requests and third parties

To retrieve public posts, RoTweet Fresh requests data from `x.pcstyle.dev` and, when needed, RSSHub public mirrors (`rsshub.app`, `rsshub.rssforever.com`, and `rsshub.pseudoyu.com`). These are third-party, unofficial services and are not operated by RoTweet Fresh. Requests necessarily identify the public username being queried and may expose ordinary request metadata, such as IP address, to those providers under their own policies. Please review the providers' policies before use.

When a user clicks an X link, the browser opens X in a new tab and X's own privacy policy applies.

## Collection and sharing

RoTweet Fresh does not operate its own server, sell personal data, or send settings to an extension developer-operated backend. It does not intentionally collect Roblox account credentials or private messages.

## Children's privacy

The extension is a utility for viewing public social posts. Parents and users should consider whether following external social-media links is appropriate for them.

## Changes and contact

This policy may be updated if the extension's data practices change. Before publishing, the extension publisher should add a monitored contact address and host this policy at a stable public URL for the Chrome Web Store listing.
